Who we are

A Lowveld legacy, cast in concrete.

Founded in 1993 by the Cason brothers, AMA Tiles has spent three decades roofing Mpumalanga's homes, estates, schools and hospitals. Our tiles are extruded under pressure for exceptional density — and uniquely, their strength keeps increasing with age.

SABS Certified Quality

Manufactured to SABS 542-2004 specification and produced under SABS ISO 9002 standards.

Built to Last a Century

Our concrete tiles can last over 100 years — and grow stronger every year they're on your roof.

Proudly Local

Made in Kabokweni and delivered across Mpumalanga within a 250–300km radius.

Roofing the Lowveld for over three decades.

From residential estates in Mbombela to landmark public projects, our tiles carry the weight of communities — honestly priced, locally cast, and made to be trusted for generations.
